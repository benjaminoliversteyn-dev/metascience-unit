UK Metascience Unit

Established in 2024, the UK Metascience Unit is a small team of policymakers, analysts and funding delivery specialists who work across UK Research and Innovation (UKRI) and the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT). The unit is co-led by Dr Jen Gold and Dr Ben Steyn.

All our work starts from a simple idea: that the scientific method, so powerful in so many areas of life, should be systematically and routinely applied to how we practice, fund and support science itself.

We have a growing cohort of 70+ projects including:

  • Multi-million-pound grant schemes, contracts, and fellowship programmes to cultivate a thriving UK metascience field, generating evidence to inform a wide range of research policy topics.
  • In-house experiments on UKRI's funding processes.

Our focus 2026–28 is 'Science Systems in an AI Age', which includes:

  1. Research peer review, evaluation and decision-making in an age of AI
    • Efficient and scalable human review to address rising application volumes (e.g. distributed peer review)
    • Testing feasibility and effectiveness of AI in assessment processes (e.g. grant, journal, national and sub-national portfolio evaluation)
    • Enhancing UKRI's internal data and analytical infrastructure to guide decision-making
  2. Building the 'Science of AI4Science': How is AI changing science?
    • National AI for Research Survey
    • Dedicated Fellowships and Grant calls
  3. Scientometrics: advancing the state of the art, while critically examining, quantitative measures of research quality beyond citations. E.g. indicators of novelty, impact, or rigour.

Programme Board

Since Jan 2024, the Metascience programme has been governed by a joint DSIT/UKRI Board, co-chaired by ESRC Executive Chair Stian Westlake and DSIT Dir. Science, Research and Innovation, Holly Yates.

Technical Support Consortium

The Unit has forged a long-term partnership with a consortium of researchers led by RAND Europe, the Science Policy Research Unit at the University of Sussex, and the Policy Institute at King's College London, under the leadership of Dr Susan Guthrie. They support the design, delivery, and quality assurance of many of the Unit's studies.
